# Idea engine: Jun Yuh

Built from the lane B notes and every Content ideation section across all 34 sources. The toolkit the seat hands a business owner who has 30 to 60 minutes a week. Lines that adapt his creator method for a business are marked adapted from the source id.

## 1. Idea sources an owner can run today

1. **Last-week log.** Write down one thing you did at work this week, then the reaction, choice or surprise inside it (adapted from S002).
2. **Three whys.** Take that moment and ask why three times: the obvious reason, the feeling underneath, the part you do not say aloud (S006, S028).
3. **Customer questions.** Copy every question from this week's DMs, comments and story replies into your bank (S024, S029).
4. **Sales calls.** After a consult, write down what the client said they are going through, in their words (adapted from S032).
5. **Mistakes list.** List the mistakes you made building the business; each one is a problem story (adapted from S004).
6. **Process notes.** Note one step in your service and why you do it that way (S006).
7. **30-minute transformation list.** Set a timer and list 20 changes you or your clients went through, then group them into themes (S014, S029).
8. **Younger customer.** Picture the customer two to three years before they found you and list their pains (adapted from S003, S020).
9. **Morning extraction.** Before you scroll, dictate ideas for five minutes (S030, S001).
10. **Competitor comments.** Read the comments on a competitor's best posts and note the unanswered questions (S029).
11. **Format study.** Pick three accounts you admire, three top posts each, and note the hook, story and visuals (S019).
12. **Old posts.** Reread a past post and ask which other questions it could answer (S002).

## 4. Weekly plan for one hour a week

**Once, about 90 minutes:** write one message and three pillars plus a story pillar (S018, S020); run the 30-minute transformation list (S014); film a B-roll bank of five to six clips per category at your workplace (S017); pick one home base scene you can shoot in five minutes (S023, S025).

**During the week, zero extra time:** log moments in phone notes as they happen (S002, S028) and grab short clips of normal work (S025).

**The weekly hour, one sitting per stage** (adapted from S017, S030):
- 10 minutes: mine. Pick three ideas from the bank and DMs.
- 15 minutes: script all three in one sitting, each as problem, pursuit, payoff (S004, S006).
- 20 minutes: film at the home base, one line at a time (S001).
- 15 minutes: edit as low intensity, such as one B-roll with hook text and value in the caption (S013, S017).

**What the three posts are** (adapted from S018, S030): one story post to attract, one value or process post to position, and one rotating slot that is reach (a past winning format plus your message) most weeks and an offer post at most once a week (S029).

**Repurposing:** turn each post into story frames across the week and one into a carousel (S013); repost a winner after about two months (S032); keep 80 percent proven formats and 20 percent experiments (S030).

**Review, five minutes:** check weekly average views; if two weeks in a row are down, look at the hook and skip rate (S017).

## 5. Nothing to post today

1. Open the bank; never start from a blank page (S002).
2. Pick the most boring or hardest moment of today and run the three whys (S030, S006).
3. Answer one customer question from your DMs or comments (S024, S029).
4. Post a caption video: one clip, hook text on top, value in the caption (S013).
5. Repost a winner from two months ago, or change its hook or clip order (S032, S024).
6. Reuse a past format that worked with a new message (S016, S019).
7. Share that it was a hard week and what you are doing about it (S012).
8. Run a story poll on an idea instead of a full post (S032).
9. Post something low intensity rather than skip the day (S016).
