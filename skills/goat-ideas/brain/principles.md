# Principles: Jun Yuh

Built from the six verified note batches covering S001 to S034, weighted to the content-ideas lane for busy business owners. 26 decision rules, most-used first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Where his view changed, both versions are dated and the newer is marked.

1. **Do not niche down; pick a message and let topics branch from it.** "A message is significantly better than a traditional niche because you're not stuck in one box." (S011) "the traditional way of niching, I believe, is outdated. It's dead." (S018) **View changed:** in May 2023 he said not to perfect the niche before starting but granted "there certainly are some benefits to finding Clarity early on with your Niche without a doubt" (S025), and that you can specify it later (S024). **Newer, Dec 2025 to 2026:** niching is dead; you are the niche. For an owner the business already sets the category; the rule applies to topics inside the content, which should range across craft, thinking and story (inferred from S006, S030). Sources: 11 (S004, S006, S011, S015, S018, S019, S023, S024, S025, S027, S030). Confidence: high.

2. **Lead with a specific problem or struggle, never with your wins.** "So, lead with pain, lead with struggle." (S030) "the issue with your guys is expert led content is you're starting off with your successes" (S011) Sources: 7 (S006, S011, S014, S019, S020, S029, S030). Confidence: high.

3. **Never start from a blank page or wait for a big event; mine the ordinary week.** "The hardest part as a creator is to actually start from complete a blank canvas." (S002) "in your most boring of times, in your most mundane moments, in your hardest days, that's when you should start creating content" (S030) Sources: 6 (S002, S006, S013, S025, S028, S030). Confidence: high.

4. **Thoughtful volume beats waiting for the perfect idea.** "you give yourself enough quantity you will get quality" (S023) "but it has to be thoughtful quantity to derive quality. It can't just be quantity." (S030) Sources: 6 (S023, S025, S027, S029, S030, S032). Confidence: high.

5. **Quality is what the customer perceives, not production level.** "Quality has nothing to do with production level. It has nothing to do with how fancy your edits are." (S017) "I truly believe quality is derived from what your audience wants" (S032) He filmed for years on a shattered phone (S024, S025). Sources: 5 (S017, S019, S024, S025, S032). Confidence: high.

6. **Study format, not substance; copy the framework, never the whole video.** "We're studying format, not substance." (S020) "you don't just take a piece of content that works well and imitate it completely" (S032) Sources: 5 (S001, S019, S020, S030, S032). Confidence: high.

7. **The audience's questions are the idea bank; go and ask them.** "But, ask ask your audience. Ask them exactly what it is that they want and do as many lives that you can do." (S029) "these are opportunities okay this means comments DMS story replies lives" (S024) Sources: 5 (S006, S022, S024, S029, S032). Confidence: high.

8. **Pick a cadence you can keep; three a week is the floor, daily is his ideal.** "I encourage everyone in here to actually post at least three times per week." (S018) "If that means four times a week, five times a week, that's just fine." (S019) "Whereas on Instagram, I often find the sweet spot is about once per day." (S030) Sources: 5 (S006, S018, S019, S029, S030). Confidence: high.

9. **Double down on winners and repost them without guilt; it never feels redundant to the audience.** "When it does work, you should actually double down on it and repurpose its format and messaging." (S015) "it's going to feel redundant to your audience, but it never does." (S030) Sources: 5 (S015, S027, S029, S030, S032). Confidence: high.

10. **Track inputs and weekly averages, not single posts or daily numbers.** "you're tracking your progress specifically for input based metrics." (S034) **View changed:** 2023: "literally never look at my own numbers" (S023). **Newer, Dec 2025:** "All I will do is take weekly averages." (S017) Sources: 5 (S017, S023, S031, S032, S034). Confidence: high.

11. **Post every day without creating every day: batch and repurpose.** "I don't create every day. I post every day." (S019) "although I was posting every single day, I wasn't creating every day." (S017) Sources: 4 (S013, S017, S019, S030). Confidence: high.

12. **AI finds, trims and challenges; it never writes the idea or the story.** "AI should never write your stories for you because the moment it does, it stops becoming yours." (S006) "I think that people that are using AI to generate them ideas are the ones that are going to fall flat very quickly." (S030) Inconsistency to note: in S029 he also asks AI to "optimize this caption" and "optimize this script", so editing help is allowed, generation is not (inferred from S001, S029, S030). Sources: 4 (S001, S006, S029, S030). Confidence: high.

13. **Be specific: one post, one problem, one theme.** "I want you to speak as though the person watching your video needs help with one specific problem." (S003) "So if you're going to make boring generic content, it it'll never win especially with how many people are doing that same thing." (S002) Sources: 4 (S002, S003, S006, S014). Confidence: high.

14. **Do not sell in every post; plant seeds and judge conversion over weeks.** "converting every single post, but they forget that there's a real human being on the opposing side of the screen. You have to earn that goodwill." (S029) "they think this one piece of content should drive me x number of sales and if it doesn't then I failed." (S030) He cites the marketing rule of seven (S010). Sources: 4 (S010, S027, S029, S030). Confidence: high.

15. **Flop on purpose; keep a slice for experiments.** "if we don't flop at least once a week, we're not trying hard enough." (S030) "If a video flops, who cares?" (S002) Sources: 4 (S002, S003, S016, S030). Confidence: high.

16. **Systems beat motivation; build the routine that runs when you do not feel like it.** "When motivation dies out, there's only systems that remain." (S033) "at its fundamental core consistency is focusing more on systems and less on motivation" (S023) Sources: 4 (S023, S029, S033, S034). Confidence: high.

17. **People buy into people: the owner's face and story come before the business page.** "People do not buy your product first. Listen to me very carefully. People buy into people." (S004) "95% of people out there don't need a business page for the first million dollars." (S030) "If you built a personal brand and then funneled the traffic into your business page, both of them would grow." (S006) Sources: 3 (S004, S006, S030). Confidence: high.

18. **Never skip the method; the pursuit is what proves you and what you sell.** "a lot of people tend to skip the pursuit. And that feels more like a flex than it does actually helpful content." (S006) "Do the three Ps. Three Ps in every single one of your posts." (S004) Sources: 3 (S004, S006, S011). Confidence: high.

19. **Make every post about the viewer's payoff, not about you.** "they never understand the content isn't about you it's about the other person that's watching a content" (S032) "A selfless piece of content is think about what is the payoff for the audience." (S018) Sources: 3 (S013, S018, S032). Confidence: high.

20. **After a hit, do not raise your standards; when tired, drop to low intensity instead of taking a break.** "their entire standards start to advance way too quickly." (S016) "The only thing that happens when you take these breaks are that your plateaus increase in its duration." (S016) Sources: 3 (S013, S015, S016). Confidence: high.

21. **Trends must carry your message; lean on proven formats over chasing what is new.** **View changed:** May 2023: "number 10 I wouldn't miss out on Trends" (S024), though even then he warned against relying only on trends. **Newer, 2026:** "Trends have to be yours." (S029) and 80% proven formats (S030), using "sounds that have been popular probably a year and a half ago." (S030) Sources: 3 (S024, S029, S030). Confidence: high.

22. **Organic content over paid reach for an owner without a big budget.** "You going to use ads? Great. Are you very rich? If not, good luck." (S004) "The only other option that you have are paid ads and that's expensive." (S006) Never buy fake reach: "never and I mean never pay for inorganic reach especially when you're starting out" (S025) Sources: 3 (S004, S006, S025). Confidence: high. Note: he sells content education, so his case against ads is not neutral (inferred from S004, S006).

23. **Tell your own evolution, never someone else's story.** "You never want to tell stories about someone else." (S002) "every time I tell a story, it is always my own spiritual or emotional maturation that has come from it. It is never theirs cuz it's not my story to tell." (S028) Tension: his own selling leans hard on student case studies and buyer shout-outs (S004, S006, S010), so client results with permission are fair game; personal details of others are not (inferred from S004, S010, S028). Sources: 5 (S002, S006, S020, S028, S029). Confidence: high.

24. **Show the why behind the work, not just the work.** "just don't teach only about the what show the why and that why is ultimately your story" (S006) "You're doing the same thing that every other expert in the space is doing." (S004) Sources: 2 (S004, S006). Confidence: medium.

25. **Batch about a week ahead, never a month.** "it should be about a week proactively. It shouldn't be more than that because content changes all the time" (S017) "I've seen people do an entire month's worth of content within a day I don't recommend that because if for some reason that Day falls apart for any reason you're probably losing out on a lot of content" (S023) Sources: 2 (S017, S023). Confidence: medium.

26. **Every hook is a promise; pay it off or lose the next view.** "A brilliant hook followed by a weak video teaches people not to trust your next hook." (S003) "You can't do a clickbait hook and not actually fulfill upon your promise." (S003) Sources: 1 (S003). Confidence: low.
