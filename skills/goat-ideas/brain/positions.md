# Positions: Jun Yuh

Built from the batch notes on all 34 sources, weighted to what a business owner asks about content. 38 topics. Each stance has a verbatim quote, source id, and date (YouTube upload month; guest interviews S028, S029 and S032 are undated). Where his view moved, the versions sit side by side with dates and the newer one is marked. Topics with no evidence say so.

## Niche
- **Stance:** Do not niche down; put a message in a box, not a topic, and let several pillars hang under it. "A message is significantly better than a traditional niche because you're not stuck in one box." (S011, 2026-04)
- **Over time:**
  - 2023-05: niche later, not never. "I wouldn't spend too much time trying to perfect my Niche before beginning" (S024) and "there certainly are some benefits to finding Clarity early on with your Niche without a doubt" (S025)
  - 2025-12: "the traditional way of niching, I believe, is outdated. It's dead." (S018)
  - 2026-08 (newer): "niching down is the most antihuman thing that you can do because we're not one thing" (S006)
- **Limit he grants:** "While this works for some people, for most people, you'll grow out of that one strict niche as you evolve." (S019, 2025-11)
- **Read for owners:** the business already fixes the topic, so his advice lands as message plus pillars, with the founder's story as one pillar (inferred from S018, S029, S030).

## Personal brand versus a business page
- **Stance:** Build the founder's personal account first and funnel it into the business page. "If you built a personal brand and then funneled the traffic into your business page, both of them would grow." (S006, 2026-08)
- If you only post as the business: "it's not because you suck at content. It's because you're making the wrong thing." (S006, 2026-08)
- If you must start on the business page, keep it founder-led: "the ratio would be like more like three personalled content but it still has to be continuously about the business like about you as a founder" (S006, 2026-08)
- Stronger version: "95% of people out there don't need a business page for the first million dollars." (S030, 2026-01) His own company page is run by staff at about five posts a month (S030).
- Faceless accounts: "We do not work with any faceless accounts. It is a very different strategy and it is personally not something I'm a fan of." (S019, 2025-11)

## Storytelling versus tips and expertise
- **Stance:** Tips alone make you one of many experts; the story behind the method is what makes people pick you. "You can't just become an educator. You have to tell your stories." (S004, 2026-08)
- "You're doing the same thing that every other expert in the space is doing." (S004, 2026-08)
- For service providers the result stays the same and the story adds the reason: "the result, the payoff remains the same, but now there's a reason to care" (S004, 2026-08)
- "People aren't short of information, they're short on connection." (S004, 2026-08)

## Where ideas come from
- **Stance:** Running out of ideas is a seeing problem, not a life problem. "it's not about living a more interesting life or doing more things. It's about recounting in different ways the things that you've already undergone." (S002, 2026-10)
- "probably 90% of the population does the exact same thing." (S013, 2026-02)
- Start from ordinary or hard days, never from waiting for a big event: "in your most boring of times, in your most mundane moments, in your hardest days, that's when you should start creating content" (S030, 2026-01)
- Never start blank: "The hardest part as a creator is to actually start from complete a blank canvas." (S002, 2026-10)

## Posting cadence
- **Stance:** Daily is his practice; three a week is the floor he gives beginners.
- **Over time:**
  - 2023-05: "number four now this is something that I would get worked up on I would not miss a day of posting" (S024)
  - 2025-11: "If that means four times a week, five times a week, that's just fine." (S019)
  - 2025-12: "I encourage everyone in here to actually post at least three times per week." (S018)
  - 2026-01: "Whereas on Instagram, I often find the sweet spot is about once per day." (S030)
  - 2026-08 (newer): "You just need to post once per day." (S006)
- Posting daily does not mean creating daily: "I don't create every day. I post every day." (S019, 2025-11)
- **Read:** for a busy owner, three a week from one batch is the version he would accept (inferred from S018, S029, S013).

## Time an owner should spend
- **Stance:** Small, fixed blocks work once you have templates. "she's only spending 15, 25 minutes a day and she's able to make a content piece because she knows the templates" (S006, 2026-08)
- 2023-05: "you should spend 30 minutes to an hour brainstorming Concepts and filming right" (S024)
- "if I have 10 minutes one day, I got to make use of those 10 minutes and make it count." (S028, undated)

## Batching
- **Stance:** Split script, film and edit into separate blocks, about a week ahead, never a month in a day.
- "I like to script my content in one sitting and then I like to film my content in another and I like to edit my content in the third block throughout the week." (S017, 2025-12)
- "it should be about a week proactively. It shouldn't be more than that because content changes all the time" (S017, 2025-12)
- "I've seen people do an entire month's worth of content within a day I don't recommend that because if for some reason that Day falls apart for any reason you're probably losing out on a lot of content" (S023, 2023-09)
- The failure mode: "Right now, you're attempting to script, film, edit all on the same day, and you're increasingly becoming frustrated with the process." (S013, 2026-02)

## Repurposing and reposting
- **Stance:** Repost winners without guilt; your audience does not see repeats the way you do. "it's going to feel redundant to your audience, but it never does." (S030, 2026-01)
- **Over time:**
  - 2023-05: "just repost your content from three weeks prior that you made" (S024)
  - Undated, later practice: "I always give my videos 2 months before I repost a video because it gives a new life while not feeling redundant" (S032)
  - 2026-01 (newer): cross-posting the same file has stopped working. "In the beginning, you used to be able to repurpose across and you can just fine." (S030) Meta catches lazy reposts: "even if you change the hook and majority of your bureau stays the same and your text stay the same, they'll crack it." (S030)
- "I want to give you permission to do this because it's actually really powerful as a creator." (S015, 2026-02)

## Production quality and gear
- **Stance:** Quality is what the audience values, not polish. "Quality has nothing to do with production level. It has nothing to do with how fancy your edits are." (S017, 2025-12)
- "I truly believe quality is derived from what your audience wants" (S032, undated)
- "I grew my first 3 million followers with a shattered iPhone 12." (S030, 2026-01)
- "Because this piece of content took me about 15 minutes to make and it got 11.9 million views." (S019, 2025-11)

## Showing your face
- **Stance:** Camera fear is normal and not a blocker; start with non-talking formats. "It does not require you to speak in front of the camera. It solely relies on B-roll, text, and music, which all of you guys can do." (S014, 2026-02)
- Then add talking videos, because they build trust a non-talking video cannot (S017, 2025-12).

## Studying formats
- **Stance:** Copy structure, never substance. "We're studying format, not substance." (S020, 2025-11)
- "No one owns a format. Everyone owns a message and that's what's unique to them." (S019, 2025-11)
- "you don't just take a piece of content that works well and imitate it completely" (S032, undated)
- Cadence of study: "I do this practice called the 7 by7. And every single two weeks I'll study 49 pieces of content." (S030, 2026-01) It is a phase, not forever: "if I can help you study content for about four weeks, I don't think you have to study that intensely afterwards" (S030, 2026-01)

## Hooks
- **Stance:** A weak post is usually packaging, not the idea. "This is often a packaging problem rather than an idea problem." (S003, 2026-10)
- "Your hook earns attention and the rest of your video earns trust." (S003, 2026-10)
- "You can't do a clickbait hook and not actually fulfill upon your promise." (S003, 2026-10)
- "The strongest hooks make people feel understood before they feel impressed." (S003, 2026-10)

## Weekly content mix
- **Stance:** Every post gets one job, and the week is a mix, not seven of the same.
- **Over time:**
  - 2025-12: "And those three are going to be number one storytelling, number two, a value post, and number three a reach post." (S018)
  - 2026-01 (newer): "So three attract, one nurture, two position, and one convert on a weekly basis for your reels." (S030)
  - Undated: the split moves with the goal. "this is definitely going to differ based on who you are and what goal you have" (S029)
- Effort mix: "For me, I always recommend that you should go one to two medium to high intensity formats and five low inensity formats." (S015, 2026-02)
- Proven versus new: "I always think like 80% should be proven formats, 20% could be experimentation" (S030, 2026-01)

## Content pillars
- **Stance:** One message on top, a few pillars under it, and your story is always one of them. The names and count keep changing.
- **Over time:**
  - 2025-12: "Three of those content pillars should be interests of yours. And then that fourth is your story." (S018)
  - 2026-02: "You have your skill, you have your mindset, your lifestyle, and your passions." (S015)
  - 2026-04: "Your message is going to be your skills, where you can monetize, perspective, what unique ideas and perspectives do you hold, and lifestyle, what do you do in your daily life?" (S011)
  - 2026-07: "One skills, two passions, three story, aka what you know, what you love and who you are." (S007)
  - 2026-10 (newest): story areas as pillars, "that's my storytelling wheel. Love, identity, fitness, and earnings." (S001)
- **Read:** treat the labels as loose; the constant is message, three or four pillars, story included (inferred from S001, S015, S018, S030).

## Trends
- **Stance:** A trend only counts if your message rides on it; proven formats now matter more than fresh trends.
- **Over time:**
  - 2023-05: "number 10 I wouldn't miss out on Trends" (S024), with "it's your responsibility to add a Twist to the content so that your personality that your brand is shown in that content" (S024)
  - Undated: "Trends have to be yours. You can't just copy and paste a trend. You have to add your own messaging or your own purpose or sentiment to it" (S029)
  - 2026-01 (newer): even sounds can be old. "But I use sounds that have been popular probably a year and a half ago." (S030)

## AI
- **Stance:** Use AI to find patterns and blind spots in your own thinking, never to write the story or hand you ideas.
- "I will never have AI write my story for me or to write a script for me." (S001, 2026-10)
- "I think that people that are using AI to generate them ideas are the ones that are going to fall flat very quickly." (S030, 2026-01)
- "AI is really good at seeing holes in your own workflow, in your own way of thinking, in your own even assumptions of your audience." (S029, undated)
- "your story is the one asset AI cannot fake, buy, or steal from you" (S006, 2026-08)

## Paid ads
- **Stance:** Ads are the expensive substitute for organic trust. "You going to use ads? Great. Are you very rich? If not, good luck." (S004, 2026-08)
- "The only other option that you have are paid ads and that's expensive." (S006, 2026-08)
- Never buy reach: "never and I mean never pay for inorganic reach especially when you're starting out" (S025, 2023-05)
- **Inconsistency:** his own launches include ad shoots and VSLs (S001, S005), so he uses ads on top of organic, not instead of it.

## Metrics and analytics
- **Stance:** Track inputs and simple weekly averages; judge success by business results, not followers.
- **Over time:**
  - 2023-09: "literally never look at my own numbers" (S023)
  - 2025-12: "All I will do is take weekly averages." (S017) Two down weeks in a row means check retention or skip rate (S017).
  - 2026-01 (newer): business first. "how much is created college making? Are we making the impact in the right spaces?" (S030)
- Inputs over outputs: "you're tracking your progress specifically for input based metrics." (S034, 2025-09)

## Flops and failure
- **Stance:** Flopping is part of the method. "if we don't flop at least once a week, we're not trying hard enough." (S030, 2026-01)
- "My videos flop all the time but you have to learn from it so that you don't make those same mistakes twice." (S003, 2026-10)
- "If a video flops, who cares?" (S002, 2026-10)

## Burnout and low-energy weeks
- **Stance:** Do not stop; drop to low-intensity content. "Just go into low inensity contents." (S016, 2025-12)
- Cause: "these creators that quoteunquote burn out, it's not because the work is extremely hard. It's because they start to have these artificial standards in their brain." (S016, 2025-12)
- **Inconsistency:** "Have I ever felt burnt out? The truth is no." (S030, 2026-01), yet he describes a sprint where he felt disconnected and then "we started doing a better job delegating my workload from that point forward." (S028, undated)

## Selling inside content
- **Stance:** Sell rarely and plant seeds weekly; one post is not a sales test. "converting every single post, but they forget that there's a real human being on the opposing side of the screen. You have to earn that goodwill." (S029, undated)
- "they think this one piece of content should drive me x number of sales and if it doesn't then I failed." (S030, 2026-01)
- "if you can plant the seeds very consistently across a month, you should have sales every single day." (S030, 2026-01)
- "People do not buy your product first. Listen to me very carefully. People buy into people." (S004, 2026-08)

## Launches and urgency
- **Stance:** A launch is a multi-day process with real urgency. "What a launch really is is that it's a full process." (S010, 2026-04)
- "In terms of urgency, there's obviously limitations around being rooted in truth." (S010, 2026-04)
- "There's that rule of seven in marketing, which states that someone needs to see your product seven times before making a purchasing decision." (S010, 2026-04)
- "A lot of people sell out, but they never tell publicly to your audience that you've sold out. You need to. These are all positioning plays." (S010, 2026-04)

## Pricing
- **Stance:** Ladder offers by how much help is included, and match content to price. "Ultimately, it's something like you do it yourself, we do it with you or we do it for you." (S030, 2026-01)
- "It's important that you recognize this so you're not doing a low-ticket strategy for a high-ticket product and vice versa." (S010, 2026-04)
- He added a $349 entry product under programs that were "several thousands of dollars" (S006, 2026-08).
- Admits weakness: "I didn't do so well in terms of pricing or didn't do so well in terms of how to get sponsors or I didn't know about any of it." (S026, 2026-08)

## Discounting
- **Stance:** Uses limited, true discounts on low-ticket offers (25 percent off to the first 500, S010). "If it's a digital product, you might be using limited codes, limited spots, limited time." (S010, 2026-04)
- "we'll have a small ENT fee of the $39 that we usually discount anyway" (S032, undated)

## Audience research and validating ideas
- **Stance:** Ask before you build, cheaply. "But, ask ask your audience. Ask them exactly what it is that they want and do as many lives that you can do." (S029, undated)
- "I need something low effort because I'm busy so I posted a story of like a simple poll poll and survey" (S032, undated)
- "you have to validate the idea and social media gives you that opportunity." (S029, undated)
- Counterexample he tells on himself: "I talked about this idea of a clothing brand and it was crickets." (S029, undated)

## Who to speak to
- **Stance:** One specific person with one specific pain; psychographics over demographics. "every one thinks about demographic but I obsess over psychographic" (S032, undated)
- "trying to gear it towards the version of you two to three years younger. This is called the younger you principle" (S003, 2026-10)
- **Read for owners:** the younger-you target becomes the customer at the stage before they hired you (inferred from S003, S020, S029).

## Privacy, oversharing, and client stories
- **Stance:** Tell your own change, never someone else's story. "every time I tell a story, it is always my own spiritual or emotional maturation that has come from it. It is never theirs cuz it's not my story to tell." (S028, undated)
- "We want to storyt tell. We don't want to overshare." (S006, 2026-08)
- "Do share only what you're comfortable with and choose parts that align with your message." (S020, 2025-11)
- **Tension:** he still wants client wins and DMs shown as proof for high-ticket offers (S010), so client results are fine as proof, told from your side (inferred from S010, S028).

## Platforms, short versus long form, carousels
- **Stance:** Pick a priority platform; short form first for reps. "That's 365 iterations that you're learning from." (S030, 2026-01)
- "you're going to have to pick and choose where you want your priority to reside." (S030, 2026-01)
- "And so, I think people just making this blanket statement of YouTube is a better long-term platform, I disagree with." (S029, undated)
- Carousels: "I actually have a meta partner who has told me that carousels will continuously be pushed out in 2026 as a way to garner more exposure." (S013, 2026-02)

## Building in public
- **Stance:** Show the build weekly before you sell. "I recommend once per week on minimum that you do one of these five things that's behind me. Either a day in life, how much money has been invested, research, progress, or mistakes." (S010, 2026-04)
- "Every single launch that I've done, every multi-million dollar launch that I've done has always been on the back of" (S028, undated) building in public.
- "The majority of them, as nice as I possibly can say that, are just bad at it." (S028, undated)

## Brand deals, affiliates, and ownership
- **Stance:** Own the offer; renting your audience caps you. "That's why brand deals, affiliates, and UGC will always have a limit." (S003, 2026-10)
- "So, you are letting a brand borrow your audience." (S029, undated) He grants "it's not inherently that it's a bad thing" (S029, undated).

## Hiring, partners, and delegating content
- **Stance:** He kept ideation in his own hands for years and is only now handing it off.
- **Over time:**
  - 2025-12: "even to this day, I'm the one that ideates, films, and posts all of my Instagram and Tik Tok content." (S017)
  - 2026-01 (newer): spends about eight hours a week ideating and wants to hand it off (S030); "I've learned that hiring is a huge skill that I'm going to have to develop over time too." (S030)
- Partners: his co-founder Sean pushed him to go all in on content and to accept losing followers for the right audience (S030, S032).

## Work hours
- **Stance:** He runs extreme launch weeks and admits it. "working 20-hour days and barely sleeping." (S005, 2026-08) "It's been 3-4 hours of sleep each day." (S008, 2026-06)
- His floor for bad days: "on days that you have 20% and you give 20% you gave your 100%" (S031, 2025-01)
- **Read:** do not hand an owner his hours; hand them his blocks (inferred from S006, S017, S030).

## Competition and copycats
- **Stance:** Copying is fine with him; copycats without an audience fade. "I think there's place for all of us to win." (S006, 2026-08)
- "you start to realize they all fizzle out and that's something that Sean told me in the very beginning that I didn't believe but it's true" (S032, undated)

## Timing: is it too late
- **Stance:** No. "I'd actually say that it's still early." (S030, 2026-01) "You're not too late." (S018, 2025-12)
- "people overestimate what they can do in a month but they underestimate what 10 years could do for you" (S032, undated)

## Focus versus diversification
- **Stance:** One path and one priority platform at a time, many offers and platforms over years.
- 2025-01: "stick to one thing and do it for long enough and you'll figure it out" (S031)
- 2026-06 (newer, at scale): "You're not going to get to a $100 million with just one offer. You're not going to get to a $100 million with one specific platform." (S009)

## Side projects and day jobs
- **Stance:** Keep the day job or school and build on the side; he did content as a student and working engineer (S018). "I believe that you do not have to drop out of school." (S030, 2026-01)

## Raising money and business debt
- **No recorded position** on outside investment or business debt. His debt talk is personal: paying off family and student loans from product income (S004, S022, S031).

## Firing, remote work, culture, selling the company
- **No recorded position** on firing, remote work, or selling a company. Culture shows only in passing, such as buying his editor a new laptop (S005).
