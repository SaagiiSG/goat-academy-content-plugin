# Books: Jun Yuh

One book. His content-ideas thinking lives in his YouTube videos, talks and paid programs, which are the corpus; book text was not fetched. Summaries are in my own words.

## The Learning System (year not stated in the corpus)
- **Core thesis:** Studying well is a repeatable system, not a gift; he built it to survive a biomedical engineering degree and says it now runs his business (inferred from S022, S023, S026). He calls it his first book and says "my first book was all about learning." (S003)
- **Key frameworks (as he describes the system in videos, not quoted from the book):** evidence-based study habits for before, during and after class (S022); encoding, retrieval, spaced repetition, interleaving and rest (S023); the four-stage loop of prime, main learning event, flush (same-night review) and revise by diagram, which he applies to podcasts, shoots and product design (S026). The lane lesson he draws: document your own method, then package it.
- **Role in his business:** came after his first digital guide, reached number one on Amazon new releases, and took the business past a million dollars while he was a student (S004, S020, S031). He says it sold through his DMs (S022).
- **Taught in sources:** no source teaches the book's text. The system it is built on appears in S022, S023 and S026; sales and positioning in S004, S006, S020, S022 and S031.

## Not a book: Guide to Academic Success (digital guide)
- A 63-page PDF and his first product (S029). It is the model for his idea that the pursuit becomes your offer: "A PDF made a million dollars." (S004) He tested it with Story polls on student struggles before making it (S032) and designed it with Stories feedback on the cover and sections (S010). Sources: S004, S010, S018, S029, S030, S032.

## Business books
- He says he has read none: "I have never read a single business book" (S021). Also S004 and S026. Treat his frameworks as practitioner rules, not ideas taken from the literature.
